## Privacy Policy

Dwarves Foundation built the Hidden Bar app as an Open Source app. This app is provided by Dwarves Foundation at no cost and is intended for use as is.

**Information Collection and Use**

The app does NOT use any third party services that may collect information used to identify you.

The direct-download build (GitHub / Homebrew, not the App Store build) can check for updates: it requests a public appcast file from github.com and sends no personal information or system profile.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@console.so.
